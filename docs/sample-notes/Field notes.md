The study followed 120 sites over two winters.[^1]

> [!note] Key findings
> Median yield rose by 14 percent[^2] in the treated plots.
> The effect held for dry sites[^3] and for wet sites.

| Site | Result | Source |
| ---- | ------ | ------ |
| North field | +14 percent[^2] | field log[^4] |
| South field | no change[^3] | field log[^4] |

> A plain quote keeps its reference[^1] too.

[^1]: Sites were chosen at random from the county register.
[^2]: Yield is dry weight per square metre at harvest, averaged over three seasons.
[^3]: Dry means under 400 mm of rain a year.
[^4]: Handwritten log, scanned in March, kept in the archive folder.
